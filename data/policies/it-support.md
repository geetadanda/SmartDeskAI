# IT Support Policies

**SmartHR Solutions | Employee Handbook Draft | San Ramon, California**

> **Draft for review:** This document describes proposed company procedures. Before publication, IT and HR should add the approved service-desk URL, support hours, emergency contact, and device-management instructions. Applicable law and signed agreements take precedence.

## 1. Purpose and scope

These policies explain how employees, contractors, and other authorized users request IT help and protect company accounts, devices, and networks. Users must use company-approved systems for company information and promptly report suspected loss, compromise, or unauthorized access.

## 2. Password reset process

1. Use the company-approved identity portal or password-reset workflow. Verify identity using the prompts provided by IT.
2. If self-service recovery is unavailable, submit a ticket to the IT service desk or contact IT using the published support channel. IT may verify identity before making an account change.
3. Choose a unique password or passphrase that is not reused on personal or other work accounts. Use the company password manager where provided.
4. Do not send passwords, one-time codes, recovery codes, or authentication links to anyone—including someone claiming to be IT. IT will not ask for your current password.
5. After a suspected compromise, report it immediately, change the password from a trusted device, and follow IT's instructions to revoke sessions and secure connected accounts.

IT may require a reset when there is a credible security concern. Users must not share accounts or attempt to bypass access controls.

## 3. VPN configuration

Remote users must connect through the company-approved VPN when accessing systems that require it. Install the VPN client only from the company software portal or another source expressly approved by IT.

To set up or troubleshoot access, submit a ticket with the device type, operating system, error message, and the time the issue occurred. Do not include passwords, MFA codes, or confidential data in the ticket. Complete MFA when prompted and disconnect the VPN when it is no longer needed or when directed by IT.

Do not install unapproved VPN products, disable security controls, or route company traffic through a personal VPN to avoid company controls. If the VPN is unavailable, use only approved alternative access methods; contact IT if you cannot determine whether an alternative is approved.

## 4. Software installation requests

Submit a service-desk request before installing software for company work. Include the business purpose, product and publisher, required operating system, requested users, and any data or system access the software needs. The manager confirms business need; IT reviews security, licensing, compatibility, privacy, and support requirements.

Install approved software from the company software portal or as directed by IT. Do not use personal licenses, unapproved browser extensions, pirated software, or tools that store company information in an unapproved service. Do not grant software administrator access or disable endpoint protections without written IT authorization.

IT will communicate the decision, any conditions, and an expected installation route. An approval for one application or use does not approve other versions, integrations, or data uses.

## 5. Multi-factor authentication (MFA)

MFA is required for company accounts and services where enabled by IT. Enroll using the approved authenticator or security key instructions. Keep backup methods current and store recovery codes securely; do not store them with your password or share them.

Never approve an unexpected MFA prompt. Deny it and report repeated or unfamiliar prompts to IT immediately. If a device or security key is lost, or a phone number changes, contact IT promptly to revoke or replace the factor. IT may require additional verification before restoring access.

## 6. Guest Wi-Fi access

Visitors may use only the guest network and must accept its access terms. Employees may request guest access through the approved visitor or IT process, providing the visitor's name, sponsoring employee, and visit dates when requested.

Do not give visitors the employee Wi-Fi password, connect guest devices to internal networks, or use guest Wi-Fi as a workaround for restricted access. Sponsors are responsible for ensuring visitors follow site and information-security rules. Report suspicious network activity to IT.

## 7. Email configuration on mobile

Access company email from a mobile device only through the company-approved email application and enrollment process. A device may need a supported operating system, screen lock, encryption, current security updates, and mobile-device management before access is granted.

Employees must:

- Protect the device with a strong passcode or supported biometric lock and enable automatic locking.
- Keep the operating system and approved email application current.
- Avoid forwarding company email to personal accounts or saving company attachments in unapproved personal storage.
- Report a lost, stolen, or compromised device to IT as soon as possible so access can be suspended and company data protected.
- Follow IT instructions for remote lock, selective removal of company data, or device unenrollment.

IT will explain any management controls before enrollment. Company data may be removed from a managed application or profile when access ends or a security issue requires it, subject to applicable law and the applicable device agreement.

## 8. Reporting, support, and exceptions

Report suspected phishing, malware, lost devices, unauthorized access, or accidental disclosure to IT promptly using the published security-reporting channel. Include relevant facts, but do not delete evidence or forward suspicious links to coworkers.

Requests for exceptions must be submitted to IT with a business justification and duration. IT may approve, deny, or impose safeguards. Contact HR or IT if an accessibility need or other accommodation affects use of a required tool.
