# Payroll and Compensation Policies

**SmartHR Solutions | Employee Handbook Draft | San Ramon, California**

> **Draft for review:** The pay dates below are a proposed operating schedule and must be confirmed by Payroll before publication. Individual compensation, incentive, and equity terms are governed by applicable written agreements and plan documents. Applicable law controls.

## 1. Pay-day schedule

The proposed standard schedule is semi-monthly, with paydays on the 10th and 25th of each month. If a scheduled payday falls on a weekend or bank holiday, Payroll will publish the applicable payment date in the payroll calendar. The payroll calendar and applicable law control if a date must be adjusted.

Employees must submit accurate time records and approved time-off information by the communicated payroll cutoff. Non-exempt employees must record all hours worked, meal periods, and other required time information accurately and promptly. Working unrecorded hours is not permitted; all hours actually worked must be reported and will be paid as required by law.

Pay is delivered through the payment method on file. Employees should keep bank and contact details current in the approved payroll system and promptly report a missing or incorrect payment to Payroll. Review each wage statement and raise suspected errors as soon as possible. Corrections will be handled in accordance with applicable law.

## 2. Tax declarations and payroll withholding

At hire, employees must complete the applicable federal Form W-4 and California Form DE 4 through the approved payroll process, along with any other required forms. Employees are responsible for making accurate elections and updating withholding information when their circumstances change. Payroll can explain how to submit a form but cannot provide personal tax advice.

The company withholds and reports taxes and authorized deductions as required by law. Employees should review each wage statement for gross wages, hours where applicable, deductions, withholding, and net pay. Questions about a withholding change, wage statement, or year-end tax document should be directed to Payroll. Tax documents will be made available using the method and timing required by law.

Do not send tax forms, Social Security numbers, bank details, or other sensitive information through ordinary email or chat. Use the approved secure payroll channel and contact HR or Payroll if you are unsure whether a request is legitimate.

## 3. Salary structure

Compensation may include base pay, incentive compensation, equity awards, and benefits, depending on role, eligibility, and written terms. Base pay is set based on factors such as role responsibilities, skills, experience, market data, internal considerations, and applicable pay-transparency requirements. Pay changes require approval and written confirmation through the company's authorized process.

Employees are classified as exempt or non-exempt based on applicable law and their actual job duties. Classification determines overtime eligibility and timekeeping obligations; a job title alone does not determine status. Non-exempt employees must record all hours worked and will receive overtime pay when required by law. No manager may ask an employee to work off the clock.

Incentive plans, commissions, and bonuses are governed by the applicable written plan or agreement, including eligibility, measurement, payment timing, and any lawful conditions. Benefit offerings and employee costs are governed by the applicable plan documents.

SmartHR Solutions does not prohibit employees from discussing or disclosing their own wages or asking about another employee's wages, except where a specific legal exception applies. Employees are not required to disclose their compensation. This policy does not limit rights under applicable pay-transparency or labor laws.

## 4. Stock-option vesting

Equity awards are not automatic and are available only if approved and documented in an individual grant notice and the applicable equity plan. The grant notice and plan control the number and type of awards, vesting commencement date, schedule, exercise price, expiration, tax treatment, and consequences of leave or separation.

Some awards may use a time-based schedule, performance conditions, or other vesting terms. Do not assume a standard schedule, cliff, or exercise period unless it appears in the employee's grant documents. Vesting generally requires continued service through the applicable vesting date, subject to the plan and written agreement.

Employees should review grant documents promptly, complete any acceptance steps by the stated deadline, and consult a qualified tax or financial adviser about individual consequences. HR or the equity-plan administrator can explain administrative procedures but cannot provide personal legal, tax, or investment advice. Contact the administrator promptly after a leave or employment-status change because plan deadlines may apply.

## 5. Payroll questions and corrections

For pay-date, direct-deposit, wage-statement, withholding, or time-record questions, contact Payroll through the approved channel. Include the pay period and a description of the issue, but do not include full bank-account numbers, tax identifiers, passwords, or authentication codes in an unsecured message.

Payroll will review reported discrepancies and explain any correction or additional information needed. Employees should continue to record time accurately and meet payroll deadlines while a question is being reviewed. No employee will be retaliated against for raising a good-faith payroll concern or exercising a right protected by law.
